# Lee 2025, Robust and Scalable Autonomous Reinforcement Learning in Irreversible Environments (NeurIPS 2025)

URL: https://openreview.net/forum?id=dAAz7afWJR (PDF: https://openreview.net/pdf?id=dAAz7afWJR)
Cited by [1] for: "irreversible states" (§3.6.2).
Read: the whole provided PDF; Appendices A–D (supplementary) not available.

Key findings
- Autonomous RL (ARL): a forward policy and a reset policy are trained together to reduce human resets during training. It is a training method, not a deployment safeguard.
- Definition: irreversible states are "those from which an agent cannot recover without external intervention".
- Detection: RSA encodes behaviours with successor features over "goal-relevant features"; a state is flagged when the reset policy "loses control over goal-relevant features". The features are "predefined".
- Avoidance: a surrogate penalty for flagged states, a conservative threshold, and an early abort of forward episodes. Evidence is empirical: "We empirically found that this conservative identification helps prevent the agent from entering irreversible states in our experiments."
- The agent must enter an irreversible state to learn it: "RSA cannot prevent an agent from entering irreversible states before the associated behaviors have been encoded."
- Recovery is a human reset: RSA "triggers manual" (page break) "resets only when the agent enters irreversible states". The result is "fewer manual resets", not none.
- Setting: goal-conditioned RL in which "the state transition model is unknown"; the policy "maps the current state and the current goal" to actions.
- Experiments: Gymnasium-Robotics simulations (PointIMaze, AntOpen, HandReach, HandManipulate, with Trap variants); 5 seeds.
- Safety guarantee: none. The NeurIPS checklist says: "The paper does not include theoretical results."
- The paper suggests that "the common-sense reasoning abilities of foundation models" may help identify irreversible states. This is a future-work remark, not a result.

Conditions: S1 a goal and predefined goal-relevant features; S2 not needed; S3 not needed (continuous control); S4 the policy reads the state; S5 a training method; S6 a human resets the agent after each irreversible state.
