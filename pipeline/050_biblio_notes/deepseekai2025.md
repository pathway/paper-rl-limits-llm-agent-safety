# DeepSeek-AI 2025, DeepSeek-R1

Used for: why process reward models were not used in training (T-PRM).

Key findings
- §2.2: the authors "abstain from applying neural reward models" because "neural reward models are susceptible to reward hacking during large-scale reinforcement learning".
- Appendix G.2 gives three limits of a process reward model: "it is challenging to explicitly define a fine-grain step in general reasoning"; judging a step is hard to annotate; and "once a model-based PRM is introduced, it inevitably leads to reward hacking".
- The appendix adds that this "does not imply that these approaches are incapable of developing effective reasoning models".
