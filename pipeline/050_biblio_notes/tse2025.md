# Tse, Chandrasekar, Machado 2025, Reward-Aware Proto-Representations in Reinforcement Learning (NeurIPS 2025)

URL: https://arxiv.org/abs/2505.16217
Cited by [1] for: exploration (§3.5, with Klissarov and Machado 2023).
Read: Full text: §1–9 and Appendices A–E (broader impact, proofs, experiment details).

Key findings
- A representation-learning paper, not a safety method. It studies the default representation (DR), a reward-aware version of the successor representation.
- Theory is tabular: "we lay some of the theoretical foundation underlying the DR in the tabular case". Theorem 4.1 proves convergence of the DP update and assumes r(s) < 0 for all non-terminal states.
- The DP method "requires access to the transition probabilities under the default policy"; TD learning does not.
- The default policy "assigns non-zero probabilities to all state-action pairs".
- Avoidance is through reward only: the agent learns "to avoid low-reward regions"; "risk awareness" is measured as average reward per step.
- Experiments: grid worlds with "100-step episodes"; the shaping experiments "assume access to the eigenvectors of the SR and DR prior to training".
- Limitation stated: "restricting the scope to this setting remains a limitation of this work" (the tabular case).
- Safety guarantee: none.

- Reachability is assumed: Assumption C.1 says "it is possible to reach any state from s0 under the default policy" (used for the eigenvector results).
- Further environments: RiverSwim and SixArms (count-based exploration), four-rooms transfer; all tabular.
- The authors note that reward-aware representations are "somewhat at odds with pure exploration", given their risk-averse nature (the PDF splits this sentence around a table).
- Broader impact: the work "does not pose any foreseeable negative societal impacts".
Conditions: S1 reward only; S2 only for the DP method; S3 the theory is tabular; S4 MDP state; S5 learning; S6 episodic grid worlds.
